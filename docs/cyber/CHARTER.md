# CYBER SITUATION ROOM

An AI-native cyber-physical investigation environment built on God's Eye View. Geography, time, digital and physical infrastructure, organizations, networks, evidence, uncertainty, cyber events and relationships belong in one investigation. This is more than cybersecurity feeds on a globe.

The globe answers WHERE; the timeline WHEN; the relationship graph WHAT CONNECTS TO WHAT; the evidence system HOW DO WE KNOW. A future investigation agent will ask WHAT SHOULD WE INVESTIGATE NEXT. The original contribution is the evidence, provenance and spatial reasoning layer.

Canonical objects: Event, Entity, Geometry, Observation, Claim, Evidence, Relationship, Time, Provenance. Epistemic states: OBSERVED (measured), REPORTED (source statement), DERIVED (calculated), INFERRED (plausible), DISPUTED (credible conflicting sources), UNKNOWN (insufficient evidence).

Spatial honesty: facilities can be points; cables, routes and dependencies can be lines; service footprints and affected regions can be areas; satellites can be orbital objects. A CVE, technique, actor, network or company has no inherent coordinate. Only defensible, evidenced relationships connect them to geometry. Current topology must never become historical fact. Country outlines and basemap textures are reference context, not event-time observations.

UX: left controls, central upstream 3D globe, right investigation, bottom event/evidence timeline. Investigation sections are KNOWN, REPORTED, INFERRED, DISPUTED, UNKNOWN, EVIDENCE and NEXT QUESTIONS. Evidence is at least as important as the globe. Avoid a generic SOC dashboard.

First scenario: Kyivstar cyberattack and telecommunications outage, December 2023. Demonstrate attack → operator/network → measured Internet disruption → geography → infrastructure context → timeline → provenance → uncertainty. Research primary statements, CERT-UA, Cloudflare, IODA, PeeringDB and RIPE Atlas. Never invent data to fill gaps.

Colonial Pipeline 2021 and KA-SAT 2022 are future ontology tests, not implementation scope. Exclude Sentinel, Defender, Splunk, private telemetry, auth, multi-tenancy, case management, generic chatbot, vulnerability dashboards, production scaling and billing. Static verified data comes before live APIs; a trustworthy evidence model comes before an agent.

Credit Bilawal Sidhu and upstream contributors prominently. Keep changes small and reviewable. Operate autonomously for routine engineering; escalate only genuine authorization needs, material product forks, irreversible major architecture, or evidence gaps that would require invented facts.
