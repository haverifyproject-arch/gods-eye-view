# The Tonga connection

One mission connects Tonga's January 2022 eruption, reported cable faults, observed network collapse, documented emergency fallback and partial service recovery. It is a retrospective evidence reconstruction, not a live incident monitor.

## Sequence

1. Approach Tonga and identify the volcanic area. Its approximate marker comes from a published seismic catalog estimate.
2. Follow the real bundled route geometry toward Fiji. Its 2026 snapshot is current reference, never claimed as a surveyed 2022 cable route.
3. Step through the measured network chronology: approximately 03:00 decline, 04:14 main explosion, 05:30 near-zero traffic, 05:35 routing update spike. Initial degradation predates the main explosion; cause of that early decline remains unresolved.
4. Isolate observed evidence. Reported objects and current cable geometry leave the scene rather than masquerading as observations.
5. Restore the full account and interrogate the reported distance. The 37 km ring is illustrative scale context, not a surveyed fault boundary or a probability region.
6. Inspect emergency satellite access documented by 10 February. No exact activation timestamp, spacecraft position or link path is inferred.
7. Reach 22 February: Cloudflare reports recovery shortly after midnight; the operator announcement at 02:13 names Tongatapu and Eua. Outer-island domestic restoration remains unresolved.

## Time semantics

`validTime` governs display intervals. `observedTime` preserves published measurement scope. `timePrecision` and `timeDescription` explain approximate or publication-anchored records. Evidence persists as historical knowledge where appropriate; decline and collapse observations expire when the next traffic phase becomes active. Midnight recovery is an approximate day anchor, not a fabricated exact onset. The repair report uses its announcement timestamp, not an invented completion instant.

Source publication dates remain separate: NASA's February and the research paper's November publication can explain January events without implying those accounts were available during January.

## Geographic semantics

Volcanic and national-context markers are approximate presentation anchors. Cloudflare traffic is not measured at a pin in Nuku'alofa. Current cable routes are generalized cartography, not a seabed survey. ITU's fault caption does not establish radial versus along-cable distance, so the distance ring cannot locate the fault by intersection with a route. No vessel motion or satellite geometry is fabricated.
